02 / Freelance · client project

Forum Labs

In development · for Forum Hospital

A home sample collection and diagnostic lab booking platform for Forum Hospital. Customers find tests, book a home collection, track the phlebotomist and get secure digital reports. I'm building it end to end: the Flutter customer app, its NestJS backend and the Angular operations dashboard.

  • 01Test & package search, cart and coupons
  • 02Pincode checks and slot booking
  • 03Pay at collection by cash or UPI
  • 04Live order tracking & secure reports
  • 05Phlebotomist field screens & barcode samples
  • 06Admin: dispatch, catalog import, revenue, staff roles
Under the hood
  • Flutter app for Android & iOS, an Angular operations dashboard for hospital staff and phlebotomists, and a NestJS + Prisma API on Supabase Postgres.
  • Only the API holds secrets or touches the database — every client signs in with Supabase Auth and goes through it.
  • Healthcare-grade rules: idempotent order placement so retries never double-book, an audit log for every order, sample and report change, and logs kept free of patient data.
  • Role-based access for staff, live support chat, and Firebase push notifications on Android.
